Add videos

Add a course once. Then choose any of its units in the viewer.

  1. Drag Add to AP viewer to your bookmarks bar. Or and save it as a bookmark.
  2. Open AP Classroom, sign in, and open a video in your course.
  3. Click the saved Add to AP viewer bookmark. This viewer opens with the course's units ready to choose.

In Zen, Chrome, Edge, or Firefox, Ctrl+Shift+B shows the bookmarks bar. Run the bookmark on the AP Classroom page.
